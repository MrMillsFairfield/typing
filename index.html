Home position

  1. Rest your left fingers on A S D F and your right fingers on J K L ;
  2. Feel the little bumps on F and J. Your index fingers live there!
  3. Both thumbs hang over the space bar.
  4. Every key has its own finger. Match the colours. After each key, return home.
  5. No peeking at the keyboard. Eyes on the words!
