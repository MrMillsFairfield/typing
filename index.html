Home position

  1. Rest your left fingers on A S D F and your right fingers on J K L ;
  2. Feel the bumps on F and J. Your index fingers live there!
  3. Both thumbs hover over the space bar.
  4. Match the colours: every key has its own finger. Return home after each key.
  5. No peeking at the keyboard. Eyes on the words!
